Privacy Policy

ArrowFlux — Arrow Puzzle Game

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Introduction

ArrowFlux ("the App") is a free puzzle game developed by Ali Hyder ("we", "us", "our"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our App.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information.

ArrowFlux is a fully offline game. We do not:

  • Collect your name, email, phone number, or any personal data
  • Track your location
  • Use analytics or tracking services
  • Display advertisements
  • Require account creation or login
  • Access your camera, microphone, contacts, or files

Data Stored on Your Device

The App stores your game progress locally on your device using AsyncStorage. This includes:

  • Completed levels and star ratings
  • XP and achievement progress
  • Daily Puzzle streak and the dates you solved it, and your Endless mode stage
  • Settings preferences (sound, theme, language, reminder time)

This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any server. If you uninstall the App, this data is permanently deleted.

Third-Party Services

ArrowFlux does not use any third-party services, SDKs, or APIs that collect user data. There are no ads, no analytics, and no social media integrations.

Children's Privacy

ArrowFlux is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any information from children or any other users. The App contains no user-generated content, social features, or external links beyond this privacy policy.

Permissions

ArrowFlux works fully offline and does not require internet access. The only permission it may ask for is:

  • Notifications (optional) — used only for the daily puzzle reminder. Reminders are scheduled on your device; no push service or server is involved, and nothing is sent anywhere. You can turn reminders off in the App's Settings or deny the permission at any time.

The App also uses vibration for haptic feedback, which does not involve any personal data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
